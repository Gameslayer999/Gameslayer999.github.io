## What it does

Tempo turns the MacBook notch into a glanceable surface, in the spirit of boringNotch and NotchNook:

- **Album art** for whatever is playing (Spotify, Apple Music, a YouTube tab, a podcast), read from macOS's own now-playing info.
- **An audio-reactive visualizer** driven by a live 5-band frequency analysis of the Mac's actual audio output.
- **An agent light** that shows the most urgent state across your Claude Code sessions. Shape carries the state as well as color, so it still reads in grayscale or with color blindness.
- **A sneak peek** of the new title and artist when the track changes.
